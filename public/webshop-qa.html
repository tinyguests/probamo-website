Manual QA testing

Webshop testing: from product page to order confirmation

Lost checkouts are lost revenue. We test your shop end to end with test orders, on the devices your customers use, and report everything that stands between a visitor and a completed order.

Book a call with us See how we report

What we test

Product pages

Variants, prices, stock, images and add-to-cart on desktop and mobile.

Cart and checkout

Discount codes, shipping rules, delivery countries, taxes and address validation.

Payments

Test payments with each method you offer, including failed and cancelled payments.

Order emails

Confirmations, invoices, shipping notifications and what the customer sees after paying.

Accounts

Registration, login, password reset and order history.

Search and filters

Finding products the way customers search for them.

Questions

Will you place real orders?

We use test modes and test payment methods wherever possible. If a real order is needed, we agree it with you first.

Which shop platforms do you test?

Any platform: we test what customers see and do, whether the shop runs on a hosted platform or custom software.

Can you test before a sales campaign?

Yes. Testing before a campaign or seasonal peak is one of the most common reasons to book.

What if users add to cart but don’t buy?

That is a drop-off problem. A drop-off audit looks at why customers abandon the checkout and what to fix first.
