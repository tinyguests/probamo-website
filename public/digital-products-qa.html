Make sure every buyer actually gets what they paid for

What we test

Sales page

Buttons, prices, links and layout on desktop and mobile.

Checkout

Payment steps, discount codes, upsells and bundles, if you use them.

Delivery

Confirmation emails, download links and file access.

Mobile buyers

How it works

  1. 1

    Share access

    Send the URL, build or login details and tell us which flows matter most. We confirm scope and price before we start.

  2. 2

    We test as real users

    A person works through your product on real devices, looking for anything that breaks, confuses or blocks.

  3. 3

    You get a report

    Written findings with steps to reproduce, severity, impact and screenshots or video, typically within 2–3 working days.

Questions

Will you make a real purchase?

We use a test product, a 100% discount code or your platform's test mode, agreed with you first.

Which platforms do you test?

Any setup we can buy through as a customer. We test the buyer's journey across all your tools, not one platform's settings.

When should I test?

Before a launch, after switching tools and before a promotion or email campaign.

How long does it take?

Reports typically arrive within 2–3 working days, depending on scope.
