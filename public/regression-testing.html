Manual QA testing

Regression testing on every release

When every fix breaks something else, releases become a risk. We test your critical flows on a regular schedule, so problems are found before your users see them.

Book a call with us See how we report

How it works

Prepared scenarios

We document your critical flows once and keep the scenarios up to date as the product changes.

A fixed testing window

An agreed day and time for each release, so testing fits your schedule.

Changed areas

Exploratory testing of what changed in the release, alongside the regression scenarios.

Fix verification

Confirmation that reported issues are actually fixed.

Release summary

A short summary of findings for each release.

Trend reporting

Which areas break most often, so your team can focus effort.

Questions

What is regression testing?

Regression testing checks that existing features still work after changes, fixes or updates.

How is ongoing testing billed?

Ongoing testing is billed monthly for an agreed number of testing days. The scope is stated in the order.

Can you work alongside automated tests?

Yes. Manual regression covers what automated tests don’t: visual issues, usability and complex journeys.

How quickly do you report?

Blockers are reported as soon as they are found; the full summary follows the testing window.
