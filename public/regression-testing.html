Questions

What is regression testing?

Regression testing checks that features which worked before still work after a change. It's done before each release so updates don't quietly break existing functionality.

Why manual instead of automated?

Automated tests check exactly what they were written to check. People notice when something looks wrong, feels slow or confuses users. Manual regression works well alongside automated tests, or before you have them.

How often do you test?

On your schedule: weekly, per sprint or before each release. We agree a regular testing window.

Can we cancel?

Monthly agreements renew as agreed in your order, with the notice period stated there.
