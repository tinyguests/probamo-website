Questions

Do you test on real devices?

Yes. Testing on real phones finds problems that simulators miss, such as performance, gestures and interruptions.

How do you get the app?

Through the usual test distribution channels for iOS and Android, or a build you send us.

Can you test an app before it is published?

Yes. Testing before store submission is the best time to find blockers.

What if users install the app and then stop using it?

A drop-off audit focuses on why users leave after installing, and what to fix first.
