Mobile app testing

Mobile app testing by people who use apps the way your customers do

We install your app, go through onboarding and the flows that matter, and try what real users do — rotating the phone, losing connection, switching apps — then report what breaks.

What we test

Onboarding and sign-up

First launch, permissions, account creation and login.

Core flows

The tasks your users open the app for, from start to finish.

Payments and subscriptions

Purchases and upgrades in test mode, including what happens when they fail.

Interruptions

Calls, notifications, backgrounding the app and losing connection mid-task.

Screens and layout

Different screen sizes, orientation and text size settings.

Before release

How it works

  1. 1

    Share access

    Send the URL, build or login details and tell us which flows matter most. We confirm scope and price before we start.

  2. 2

    We test as real users

    A person works through your product on real devices, looking for anything that breaks, confuses or blocks.

  3. 3

    You get a report

    Written findings with steps to reproduce, severity, impact and screenshots or video, typically within 2–3 working days.

Questions

How do I share the app with you?

Through a pre-release channel such as TestFlight or an internal testing track, or as a build file. We can also test the store version.

Which devices do you test on?

We agree the devices and operating system versions with you when we scope the project, based on what your users have.

Can you test before release?

Yes. Testing a release candidate before store submission is one of the most useful times to do it.

How long does it take?

Reports typically arrive within 2–3 working days, depending on scope.
