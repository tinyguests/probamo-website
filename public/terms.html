We provide software testing services, including acceptance testing, manual quality assurance testing, drop-off audits and related reporting, as described on our website or in your quote.   • Provide accurate scope, URLs, test accounts, and access in a timely manner;
  • Use staging or test environments where possible, and safe test payment data for shops;
  • Ensure you have rights to grant us access to systems and content we test;
  • Review reports and decide what to fix, ship, or defer — we do not deploy your product.

4. Scope, fees and payment

Every engagement is quoted individually. The quote or order states the scope of work, including what will be tested, the environments and devices covered, and the number of testing days or hours included, together with the price. Work outside the agreed scope is carried out only after it has been agreed in writing, at the rate stated in the quote or otherwise agreed.

One-off projects

Unless agreed otherwise, 50% of the fee is paid upfront to confirm the booking, and work starts once this deposit is received. The remaining 50% is due within 7 days of the date we send you the report.

Ongoing engagements

For engagements that run over several months, the order states the billing period (monthly unless agreed otherwise) and the testing time included in each period. For the first billing period, 50% of that period’s fee is paid in advance and work starts once it is received; the remainder is due within 7 days of the end of that period. Payment for later billing periods follows the schedule in the order. Unless the order says otherwise, testing time not used within a billing period does not carry over to the next one.

If an invoice is overdue, we may pause testing until it is paid. Probamo is not registered for VAT, so no VAT is added to our prices or invoices.
