Extra testing capacity

What we test

Functionality

Regression

Why manual testing still matters

Automated tests check exactly what they were written to check. People notice when something looks wrong, feels slow or doesn’t make sense. Manual testing finds the problems users actually run into, and works well before automated tests exist or alongside them.

Our testers work from your users’ point of view, on the devices they use, and document each issue so your developers can reproduce it straight away.

What you get

  • Written findings with steps to reproduce
  • Severity and impact on users and the business
  • Screenshots or screen recordings
  • Device, browser and build for every issue
  • A short summary for decision-makers

Questions

What is manual QA testing?

Manual QA (quality assurance) testing means a person uses your software the way your customers and staff would, looking for bugs, confusing steps and anything that blocks them.

How is manual testing different from automated testing?

Automated tests check exactly what they were written to check. Manual testers notice when something looks wrong, feels slow or confuses users. The two work best together.

Can you test on a regular schedule?

Yes. Ongoing testing gives you an agreed number of testing days each month, scheduled around your releases.

What is a Health Check?

A fixed-scope review of a website, webshop, mobile app or AI-built app, with a report in 2–3 working days. It is a good first engagement.

What do you need from us?

Access to a test environment, build or URL, test accounts, and a short note on which areas matter most.
