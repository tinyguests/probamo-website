Core service

Acceptance testing

Independent verification that new software or a release does what was agreed, whether it was built by your own team or an external partner. You get a clear verdict before you sign off, pay or go live.

When to bring us in

  • Before signing off a milestone or release
  • Before go-live, while there is still time to fix things

For products that lose users

Drop-off audit

When people try a product and leave, there is usually a specific reason: a confusing step, a flow that fails on one device, or a promise the product doesn’t keep. We test your product from your users’ point of view to find it, and show you what to fix first.

Expert walkthrough

We go through sign-up, onboarding, first use and upgrade as a new user would, on the devices your users have.

Testing with real users

We recruit people who match your audience and observe where they hesitate, get lost or give up.

Analytics and recordings

Where analytics or session recordings exist, we review them to confirm where users drop off and link it to the cause.

Use one, two or all three. One report covers where users leave, why, the likely business impact, and which fixes matter most.

Questions

What is acceptance testing?

Acceptance testing verifies that new software or a release does what was agreed, before you sign off, pay or go live. We test it against your requirements and against how your staff and customers will really use it, then give a clear recommendation: ready, ready with conditions, or not ready.

What is manual QA testing?

Manual QA (quality assurance) testing means a real person uses your software the way your customers and staff would, looking for bugs, confusing steps and anything that blocks them. Automated tests only check what they were written to check; a person notices when something looks wrong, feels slow or doesn’t make sense.

What is a drop-off audit?

A drop-off audit finds out why users leave your product or never become paying customers. We test your product from your users’ point of view, combining an expert walkthrough, testing with real users and, where available, your analytics and session recordings to find where users struggle, drop off or decide not to pay, and what to fix first.

Our developers already test. Why use independent testing?

Your team knows the product best, but developers test what they built, the way they built it, and rarely have time to test end to end. Independent testers have no stake in the outcome and test from the business side: your requirements, your users and your risks.

Will this cause friction with our development team?

It usually reduces it. Issues arrive as clear, reproducible reports instead of vague complaints, so they are faster to fix and harder to dispute. You decide who sees the report; with your approval we work directly with your developers.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted individually. The quote states what will be tested, how many testing days are included and a fixed price. One-off projects: 50% upfront and the rest within 7 days of the report. Ongoing testing is billed monthly. No VAT is added.

What do you need from us to start?

Access to the software (a test environment, build or URL), test accounts, and whatever was agreed with the development team: a contract, specification, user stories or even emails. If nothing was written down, we help you capture the key expectations first.

How long does it take?

A Health Check usually takes 2–3 working days. For larger releases, the timeline is agreed upfront around your sign-off or launch date.

Where are you based?

Probamo is based in Zagreb and works remotely with clients worldwide. On-site visits and on-site testing are possible anywhere by prior agreement.
