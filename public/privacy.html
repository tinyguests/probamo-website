Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

1. Who we are

This website and our manual quality assurance testing services are run by PROBAMO, obrt za usluge i trgovinu, owned by Sara Lubura, Biševska Ulica 2, Veliko Polje, Zagreb, Croatia (“Probamo”, “we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy, which explains how we handle it when you visit our website or engage our services.

Contact: testing@probamo.com

2. Data we collect

  • Contact and inquiry data — name, email, company, and message content when you email us or request a quote.
  • Project data — URLs, credentials (if provided for testing), test accounts, documentation, and bug reports created during QA engagements.
  • Technical data — basic server logs (IP address, browser type, pages viewed) recorded by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, for security and performance.

3. How we use data

We use personal data to:

  • Respond to inquiries and deliver QA services you purchase;
  • Prepare test reports and communicate findings;
  • Invoice and fulfil contractual obligations;
  • Improve our website and protect against abuse.

Legal bases (GDPR): contract performance, legitimate interests (running our business and securing our site), and consent where required (e.g. optional marketing — if offered later).

4. Sharing and processors

We do not sell personal data. We use the following service providers (processors) to run our business:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) — website hosting, content delivery, and security. Transfers to the US are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Proton AG (Switzerland) — email. Switzerland is recognised by the EU as providing an adequate level of data protection.

Any other tools we use (for example for invoicing or file sharing) process data under data processing agreements where required. We may disclose data if required by law.

5. Retention

Inquiry emails are kept as long as needed to handle your request and for reasonable business records. Project materials and reports are retained per our agreement with you and applicable legal requirements, then deleted or anonymised.

6. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; please use test credentials and non-production environments where possible when sharing access for QA.

7. Your rights

If you are in the EEA/UK, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object, and port your data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Croatia this is the Personal Data Protection Agency (Agencija za zaštitu osobnih podataka, AZOP). Contact us at testing@probamo.com to exercise these rights.

8. Cookies

This static site does not use analytics or advertising cookies by default. If we add analytics or embedded content later, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for consent.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
