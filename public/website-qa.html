Questions

What is website QA testing?

Website QA (quality assurance) testing checks that a site works as intended before and after it goes live. Manual QA is done by people, not scripts, so it also catches confusing flows, layout problems and content mistakes that automated checks miss.

Do you test on mobile?

Yes. Layout and core flows are checked on mobile and desktop. The Advanced pack covers six device and browser combinations.

What do you need from me?

The URL, any logins or test accounts, and a short note on the pages or flows that matter most.

How long does it take?

Reports typically arrive within 2–3 working days, depending on how many pages are in scope.
