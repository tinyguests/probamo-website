Testing AI-built products

A human check for apps and websites built with AI

AI tools let you ship in days. They also produce flows that work on the happy path and fail everywhere else. We test your product like a real user and show you what to fix before customers find it.

Request a quote See an example finding

What we test

Edge cases

Empty fields, very long inputs, double clicks, the back button and refreshing mid-flow.

Accounts and logins

Sign-up, email confirmation, password reset and sessions.

Payments

Checkout and subscription flows in test mode, including failures.

Obvious access problems

Pages that open without logging in, or show data they shouldn't.

UX gaps

Dead ends, missing error messages and screens with no clear next step.

Trust details

Placeholder text, broken links, missing legal pages and emails that land in spam.

How it works

  1. 1

    Share access

    Send the URL, build or login details and tell us which flows matter most. We confirm scope and price before we start.

  2. 2

    We test as real users

    A person works through your product on real devices, looking for anything that breaks, confuses or blocks.

  3. 3

    You get a report

    Written findings with steps to reproduce, severity, impact and screenshots or video, typically within 2–3 working days.

Questions

Do you test apps made with Lovable, Bolt, v0, Cursor or Bubble?

Yes. We test the product, not the code, so it doesn't matter which tool built it.

Do you fix the bugs?

No. We're independent testers. You get a clear report that you, your developer or your AI tool can work from.

Is this a security audit?

No. We flag obvious access problems we come across, but a full security test needs a specialist.

When should I test?

Before you launch or start paid marketing, and after any big change generated in one go.
