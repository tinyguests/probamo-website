IoT, robotics and hardware software

Testing for IoT, robotics and hardware-connected software

When software controls a physical device, bugs show up as devices that won't pair, settings that don't stick and dashboards that show the wrong state. We test from the user's side: setup, control and everything in between.

What we test

Setup and pairing

First-time setup, pairing, re-pairing and factory reset flows.

Companion apps

Mobile and desktop apps that control or monitor the device.

Dashboards and portals

Web interfaces for fleets, readings, alerts and settings.

State and sync

Whether the app shows what the device is actually doing.

Connectivity

Weak signal, dropped connections and recovering afterwards.

Updates

App and firmware update flows as the user experiences them.

How it works

  1. 1

    Share access

    Send the URL, build or login details and tell us which flows matter most. We confirm scope and price before we start.

  2. 2

    We test as real users

    A person works through your product on real devices, looking for anything that breaks, confuses or blocks.

  3. 3

    You get a report

    Written findings with steps to reproduce, severity, impact and screenshots or video, typically within 2–3 working days.
