Manual QA testing

Testing software for IoT, robotics and hardware

When software controls physical devices, failures are expensive and visible. We test setup, control apps and dashboards the way operators and customers use them, remotely or on site by agreement.

Book a call with us See how we report

What we test

Setup and pairing

Unboxing to first use: pairing, onboarding and configuration.

Companion apps

Mobile and web apps that control or monitor the device.

Dashboards

Operator and admin dashboards, alerts and reports.

Connectivity

Lost connections, reconnection, offline mode and syncing.

Operational scenarios

Shift changes, many devices at once, and real-world conditions.

Updates

Firmware and app updates from the user’s side.

How we work

  1. 1

    Intro call

    Tell us about the product or project, your concerns and your deadline.

  2. 2

    Scope and quote

    We define what will be tested and how many testing days it takes, then send a fixed quote.

  3. 3

    Testing

    We test against your requirements and real use, and flag blockers and anything that could annoy or confuse your customers, or make them leave.

  4. 4

    Report and retest

    You receive the full report, we go through the findings with you on a call, and retest the fixes before sign-off.

Questions

Do you need the hardware?

Usually yes. We agree whether you send a test unit, give remote access, provide a simulator or host us on site.

Can you test on site?

Yes. On-site visits and testing are possible anywhere by prior agreement.

Do you test firmware?

We test from the user’s and operator’s side. Low-level firmware and electrical testing need a specialist lab.

What experience do you have?

Robotics and automation, micromobility, warehouse and delivery operations, alongside web and mobile apps.
